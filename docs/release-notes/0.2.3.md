Strata 0.2.3 shows what's in your files, right in the list.

## New
- **Icon previews in the list and columns.** Like Finder, each file shows a Quick Look thumbnail of its contents in
  place of its icon: photos, PDFs, movies, documents, and any type an app adds thumbnails for. Folders, apps, archives
  and links keep their icon. Thumbnails stay put while files change, and Quick Look zooms from them.
- **Show icon previews** in Settings ▸ Appearance turns them off for the list, columns and icons, like Finder's
  View Options.

## Fixed
- The **Text size** row in Settings ▸ Appearance no longer shows its label twice.
