Strata 0.5.0 puts a search box in the Dock.

## New
- **Search box in the Dock.** Turn it on in Settings ▸ General ▸ Dock (it's off until you do). A search box sits in the
  Dock right after Strata's icon, shaped like the icons around it, and works from any app, much like the one on the
  Windows taskbar. It finds what ⌘K finds (the folders you use, places, workspaces, `github/acme`-style paths, files),
  plus your apps, a few commands, and documents by what they say. Plain English works too ("pdfs from last week"),
  with a row that opens the whole search in a Strata window. Return opens a result; ⌘Return shows it in Strata.
  - Strata keeps a few empty tiles in the Dock for it and needs Accessibility access to find where they are. The box
    follows them if you drag them elsewhere, hides while the Dock is hidden, and isn't shown with the Dock on the left
    or right. Turning it off takes the tiles out again; spacers of your own are never touched.
- **⌘K understands plain English** for files, as the search field does: "pdfs from last week", "videos bigger than 1 GB".

## Fixed
- **⌘K no longer flickers as you type.** Keystrokes are taken together and the file search waits for a short pause, so
  the list stops jumping and redrawing on every key.
- **No dark ring around ⌘K.** The palette draws its own soft shadow around its rounded glass instead of the window's,
  which ringed it in dark ink while it had focus.
