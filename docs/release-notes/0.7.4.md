Strata 0.7.4 brings books and comics into the file browser with cover thumbnails, offline EPUB previews and comic page browsing with Space. It also adds Books and Comics search filters and fixes Spotlight author searches.

## New

- **Read EPUB chapters in Quick Look and the inspector.** Press Space on an EPUB to see its title, author and contents, then choose a chapter or use the previous and next controls. A compact native header shows your place while leaving room for reading. Cover pages have a readable label instead of an internal filename, and untitled sections get numbered labels. Scripts and online content stay blocked.
- **Browse comic archives as a contact sheet.** CBZ, CBR, CB7 and CBT files open in Quick Look with a grid of page thumbnails and a preview of the selected page. Browse pages without extracting the whole comic, switch between List and Icons, and copy or drag a previewed page out. Your usual Quick Look view preference stays separate.
- **Recognize books and comics by their covers.** With icon previews enabled, EPUB and comic archives can show cover thumbnails in Icons, Columns and Gallery. EPUB covers come from the book’s metadata; comic covers use the first image in natural filename order.
- **Find books and comics by kind.** Search with `kind:book`, `kind:ebook` or `kind:comic`, or choose Books or Comics in the search filters. Book searches also include MOBI, AZW3 and DjVu files; reading previews are available for EPUB.

## Fixed

- **Author searches match Spotlight’s author metadata correctly.** Queries such as `kind:book author:Asimov` now check the saved author list. Results depend on the file’s author metadata and Spotlight indexing.
