Strata 0.4.2 makes the divider between two panes stay where you drag it.

## Fixed
- **A Gallery pane keeps its width.** In dual pane, dragging the divider to widen a pane showing Gallery worked, but
  the pane sprang back narrower as soon as you let go. It now stays where you leave it.
- **Disk Usage narrows too.** A long total under Disk Usage ("Measuring… folders need Full Disk Access…") could keep
  its pane, or the window, from getting narrower. The total now shortens instead.
