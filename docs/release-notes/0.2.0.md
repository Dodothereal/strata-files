Strata 0.2.0 is the biggest update yet: the Finder features people asked for, a few power tools, 21 languages, and
updates that install themselves from now on.

> **Updating from 0.1:** download this version once and drag it to Applications, replacing the old one. After that,
> Strata updates itself through **Strata ▸ Check for Updates…**, or on its own if you let it.

## New

### Everyday Finder features
- **Trash in the sidebar.** Open it, **Put Back** (⌘⌫) items to where they came from (also ones Finder trashed),
  **Delete Immediately…** (⌥⌘⌫) and **Empty Trash…** (⇧⌘⌫). Other disks' Trashes are counted too.
- **Share** the selection with AirDrop, Messages, Mail and more, from the context menu, File menu, toolbar or palette.
- **Spring-loaded folders**: hold a drag over a folder (in any view or the sidebar) and it opens.
- **Compress and Extract.** Zip the selection; extract zip, tar, 7z, rar and more, safely (nothing can be written
  outside the folder, links can't escape, archive bombs stop before filling your disk, downloaded archives stay
  quarantined). Everything shows in Transfers with progress, Stop, undo and History.
- **Open archives like folders**, the way Windows Explorer does: double-click a zip to browse it, Quick Look things
  inside and copy them out. It's a read-only copy, cached until the archive changes. (Settings ▸ General can turn
  this off.)
- **Finder tags**: the colored dots in every view, the seven colors and Tags… in the context menu, a Tags section in
  the sidebar, and `tag:Red` in search. Tags… and the Inspector pick tags like Finder: your tags with their colors,
  narrowed as you type, and "Create New Tag" for a new name. Strata and Finder share the same tags.
- **Rename several items at once**: replace text (or a regular expression), add text, number them with a format,
  or change case, with a live preview, one undo and swaps that just work.
- **iCloud Drive and cloud folders** (Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive…): see what's only in the cloud or still
  moving, and **Download Now** or **Remove Download**.
- **Get Info, editable** in the Inspector: comments (saved as you type), the app a file opens with (or all like it),
  Locked, and who may read and write it.
- **Connect to Server** (⌥⌘K): SMB, AFP, NFS and WebDAV shares, with macOS's own login and the Keychain.
- **Gallery view** (⌘4): a big preview over a strip of thumbnails.

### Power tools
- **Compare and sync two folders** in dual pane: what's only on one side, newer, or different, then sync both ways,
  one way, or mirror (extras to the Trash). Copies are verified and the whole sync can be undone.
- **Search**: `regex:` for regular expressions, `case:` or **Match Case** for exact case, and file metadata through
  Spotlight: `camera:iphone`, `width:>1920`, `duration:>10m`, `pages:>20`, `author:`, `artist:`, plus plain-English
  searches like "photos taken with iPhone" or "videos longer than 10 minutes".
- **Custom keyboard shortcuts** in Settings ▸ Shortcuts, for every menu command.
- **Appearance** in Settings: light, dark or system, three row densities, and the file name text size.

### 21 languages
Strata now speaks English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch, Swedish,
Danish, Norwegian, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified
and Traditional), down to the permission prompts macOS shows for it. It follows your Mac's language, or pick one in
Settings ▸ General. Translations live in plain JSON files, one per language: improving one or adding yours is a pull
request away (see `Localization/README.md`).

### Updates
Strata checks for updates with Sparkle and installs them when you say so (or automatically, if you choose in Settings).
Every update and the list of updates are signed, and checked before anything is unpacked.

## Changed
- **⌘4 is now Gallery and Disk Usage moved to ⌘5**, so ⌘1–⌘4 match Finder (Icons, List, Columns, Gallery).
- **A cleaner Inspector**: every section is a card you can collapse, "Where" shows the folder by name (click it to
  go there), and the panel can be dragged wider or narrower again.
- The Inspector describes items inside an archive by where they are in it.
- History shows batch renames as renames, and every operation in your language.
