Strata 0.6.2 fixes the missing quick actions in the app’s Dock menu.

## New

- **Open a new window from the Dock.** Right-click Strata’s Dock icon and choose New Window, even when all browser
  windows are closed.
- **Jump to Recents and favorite folders.** Open Recents directly or choose a folder from the Favorites submenu.
  Favorites follow the sidebar’s folder names and order, and update each time you open the menu. These commands are
  available while Strata is running, including when another app is in front.
