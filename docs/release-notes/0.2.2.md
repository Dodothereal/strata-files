Strata 0.2.2 checks your other apps for updates too, and keeps Large Files steady.

## New
- **Check apps for updates.** Right-click an app, several, or everything in Applications, and choose
  **Check for Updates…**. Strata reads each app's own update feed, or asks the App Store, and lists which ones have a
  newer version, with the way to get it: the App Store, Software Update for Apple's apps, or the app itself, which
  installs its own update. It only goes online when you ask, and only to those feeds and the App Store.

## Fixed
- **Large Files no longer flickers.** Searches and Recents followed every change on your Mac by rebuilding the whole
  list several times a second, which kept the CPU busy. Now they refresh at most every couple of seconds, and only
  rows that actually changed are redrawn.
