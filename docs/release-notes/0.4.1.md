Strata 0.4.1 fixes Gallery in Dark Mode and tidies dual pane.

## Fixed
- **Gallery in Dark Mode.** Gallery's background could stay white (hiding the file's name) after your Mac switched
  from light to dark. It now follows the appearance.
- **Dragging the divider next to Gallery.** A pane showing Gallery held the divider between the panes; a long file
  name or the big preview kept it from narrowing. The name now shortens and the preview shrinks with the pane.
- **The line between the panes** is the same grey as the app's other lines, and starts under the toolbar instead of
  running through it.
